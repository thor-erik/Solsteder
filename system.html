Shades · Design System
Source of truth for visual decisions in Solsteder. Built around the lens metaphor: translucent panels = sunglass lenses, solid cards = objects in the lens, honey accent = sun coming through. Each surface in the app belongs to exactly one tier; the tier determines opacity, effects, motion, and accent usage.
Seven principles
These hold across every surface. Disagreements with a tier spec should be resolved against these.
01
Lens metaphor is structural
Translucent surfaces = lens. Solid surfaces = objects in the lens. Honey accent = sun.
02
One accent per surface
Honey is the "this matters" signal. Burns out when overused — use it once per screen.
03
Cohesive secondary text
Secondary text = primary at lower opacity, not a different hue. Cream-at-0.78 over slate-grey.
04
Subtle static effects
Mirror-sky for soft polish. Chromatic for angle-of-view physics. Not decoration.
05
Reactive motion
Silent at rest, alive on hover/click. Tilt + spotlight + rim only when the user shows up.
06
Layered elevation
1px contact shadow + 14px lift shadow. Inset top sheen + bottom shade. Modern glass card.
07
Warm-on-cool does the work
Honey CTAs against slate base. No need to multiply colors — the contrast is the system.
Six tiers
Every UI surface belongs to exactly one. The tier determines opacity, accent use, and motion treatment. Tiers stack: Tier 2 sits inside Tier 1, Tier 3 controls live in Tier 1 / Tier 2, Tier 4 fires once per screen.

How to use this document:

  • Adding a new component? Find the closest existing element in
    the audit table. Inherit its tier. Apply the tier's spec (opacity,
    border, shadow, motion).

  • Disagree with a tier assignment? Resolve against the seven
    principles above, not against personal taste. The principles are the
    constitution.

  • Adding a new visual style? Ask: which tier? If none fits,
    consider whether you actually need a new style or whether the existing
    tier was insufficient. Tier proliferation is the enemy.

  • Brand change? Update the primitive layer in :root;
    semantic tokens propagate; canvas tracks via js/tokens.js bridge.
    Tier specs stay constant; only the colors shift.

Motion vocabulary (across tiers):

  Tier 1 (panels)        →  No ambient motion. Reveal animation on open
                            (slide / fade). Scroll-shimmer on hero panels only.
  Tier 2 (cards)         →  Tilt + parallax + spotlight + rim + click ripple
                            (when fx active). Scroll-aware (suspends during scroll).
  Tier 3 (controls)      →  Hover spotlight + click ripple.
  Tier 4 (honey CTA)     →  Hover brightness shift + click shine sweep.
  Tier 5 / 6 (badges)    →  Idle drift on the single "live in sun" indicator
                            ONLY. All other badges static.

Anti-patterns to watch:

  ✗ Multiple Tier-4 honey CTAs in one screen — burns out the signal.
  ✗ Tier-3 control with Tier-2 effects (chromatic / mirror) — too busy for
    small surfaces.
  ✗ Tier-2 card with no border or shadow — loses silhouette, blends with panel.
  ✗ Static text in slate-grey when the title is cream — breaks principle 03.
  ✗ Continuous animation on more than ONE element per screen — Tier 5 ambient
    is privileged.
