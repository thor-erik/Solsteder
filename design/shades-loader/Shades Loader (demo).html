04

Phase reference

Pixel-aligned start and end states; ease-out throughout for a single, continuous motion read.

1 Intro

Plays the resolve in reverse. Sun rises out of the half-dome to a full circle; shadow descends behind it and derotates from diagonal to horizontal.

~350 ms · ease-out

2 Loading loop

Horizontal blinds in front of the full sun. Yellow → blue → yellow per cycle, 50 ms holds at extremes. At every yellow extreme, checks loading state; on completion, transitions immediately to Phase 3.

~2 s / cycle · ease-in-out · adaptive

3 Resolve

Sun descends into the half-dome; shadow rises behind it and rotates horizontal → diagonal. Lands precisely on the static logo. No hold.

~700 ms · ease-out

4 Crossfade

Logo + wordmark fade out together; the map view fades in underneath. Won't start until the map signals ready — the crossfade reveals a finished view, not loading tiles.

~300 ms · ease-out · gated on map-ready
