The service

Shades is a solar-optimised venue finder for Oslo. It estimates which outdoor seating areas are in sun or shade at a given time using publicly available building geometry, solar position calculations, and weather forecasts. Shades is provided free of charge for personal, non-commercial use.

Sun, shade, and weather indications are estimates based on models and third-party data. They are not guarantees. Buildings change, weather is unpredictable, and venues open and close. Always check ahead.
