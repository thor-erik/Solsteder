Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Solsteder is a solar-optimised venue finder for Oslo. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data controller

Thor Erik Norbom is the data controller for this service.
Contact: contact@findshades.app

What we collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, and profile picture via Google OAuth. This is stored in our Supabase-hosted database solely to identify your account and personalise the experience.
  • Venue suggestions — if you suggest a venue, we store the venue name, coordinates, and your account identifier so we can attribute the suggestion and let you manage it from your profile.
  • Venue edits — any edits you propose to existing venue data (opening hours, seating details) are stored alongside your account identifier for review and attribution.

Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data under the following legal bases (GDPR Article 6):

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — when you sign in with Google, you explicitly consent to us receiving and storing your profile information. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — we process venue suggestions and edits to operate and improve the service. Our legitimate interest is providing accurate venue data to all users.

What we do not collect

  • We do not run any advertising or third-party tracking scripts.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not store precise device location — location is used only in your browser to centre the map and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • We do not use cookies beyond what Supabase Auth sets for session management.

Third-party services

The app uses the following external services, each with their own privacy terms:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (EU region). Privacy policy
  • Mapbox — map tiles and rendering. Privacy policy
  • Yr / MET Norway — weather forecast data. No personal data is sent.
  • Google OAuth — sign-in only. We do not access your Google data beyond the profile fields listed above.

International data transfers

Your account data is stored in Supabase's EU region. However, some third-party services (Mapbox, Google) are based in the United States. These transfers are safeguarded by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as applicable. No personal data beyond what is described above is transferred to these services.

Cookies

Solsteder uses only strictly necessary cookies set by Supabase Auth for session management. These are required for the login feature to function and are exempt from consent requirements under the ePrivacy Directive. We do not use any analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies.

Data retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account exists. Venue suggestions you withdraw are marked as withdrawn and excluded from the public dataset but may be kept for audit purposes.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — delete your account and all associated data by contacting us.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — you can sign out and request account deletion at any time.
  • Withdraw suggestions — you can withdraw any pending venue suggestion from your profile page.
  • Lodge a complaint — if you believe your data rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) at datatilsynet.no.
