Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 May 2026

Solsteder is a solar-optimised venue finder for Oslo. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

This service is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data controller

Thor Erik Norbom is the data controller for this service.
Contact: contact@findshades.app

What we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, and profile picture (avatar) via Google OAuth. We store these in a profiles row tied to your unique user ID, along with your role (regular user, editor, or admin) and language and unit preferences. This is used to identify your account and personalise the experience.

User-generated content

When you actively create content in the app we store it tied to your account identifier:

  • Suggested venues — name, location, notes, and review status.
  • Venue edits — proposed changes to opening hours, seating geometry, or other venue attributes.
  • Favorites — the list of venues you have starred.
  • Sun alerts — the venues and time windows you have set up notifications for.
  • Plans — venue, date and time, optional notes, and the list of friends you invited.
  • Plan responses — your accept/decline status and optional arrival time on plans others have invited you to.
  • Check-ins — venue + timestamp, visible to your friends. Check-ins automatically expire after three hours.
  • Friend invite tokens — when you copy a one-click share-link to add a friend, we mint a single-use token tied to your user ID. The token contains no personal data, is unguessable (122 bits of entropy), expires after 30 days, and is consumed the first time someone clicks the link.

You can withdraw pending suggestions, remove favorites, cancel sun alerts, delete plans you host, decline invites, and end check-ins yourself from the app.

Usage data (analytics)

We log a small set of in-app events to a first-party analytics table in our own Supabase database. Events are linked to your account identifier when you are signed in; otherwise they are tied only to an in-memory session ID that resets every page load. We never use this data for cross-app or cross-site tracking and do not share it with advertising networks.

Events we log include:

  • Session lifecycle — session start, page hide, page unload.
  • Map and time interactions — map movement, day navigation, time changes, calendar opens, area filter changes, sort changes.
  • Venue interactions — venue impressions, venue views, detail panel opens and closes, share actions.
  • Search — the search terms you type into the search box.
  • Location — whether you granted or denied browser location access, and when you tapped the “locate me” button.
  • Social features — plan invite responses and plan preview opens.
  • Editor — when you open the venue editor and submit a proposed edit (editors and admins only).

Each event includes:

  • Session ID (in-memory UUID, resets on every page load).
  • Your user ID, only when you are signed in.
  • Event-specific properties (e.g. which venue, which filter).
  • A device snapshot: screen size, viewport size, language, mobile flag, referring URL.
  • UTM parameters from the URL, if present.
  • Timestamp.

Location data

If you grant browser location permission, your precise location is used inside the browser only — to centre the map, sort venues by distance, and show a “you are here” dot. We do not store or transmit your precise GPS coordinates to our servers. We log whether you granted or denied the permission (so we can measure how the location feature is used), but not the coordinates themselves.

Social features

If you use the app’s social features we store the following so the feature can work:

  • Friend graph — an entry per friendship with the two user IDs involved and a status (pending, accepted, blocked).
  • Plan invites — the user IDs of people you invite to a plan and their response status. Invitees can see the host’s name and avatar.
  • Check-ins — the venue you checked in at and the timestamp, visible to your accepted friends. Auto-expires after three hours.

Anyone you have not added as a friend cannot see your friend list, plans, or check-ins. A logged-out visitor following an invite link only sees the inviter’s first name and the venue.

Push notifications

If you enable push notifications, we store a per-device push identifier so we can send you notifications when, for example, a friend invites you to a plan or your saved sun window is starting:

  • On the web, your browser issues a push subscription endpoint (a URL provided by Apple, Google, or Mozilla’s push service) together with two cryptographic keys.
  • In the iOS and Android apps, the operating system issues a device push token (Apple Push Notification service on iOS, Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android). This token identifies your device to the push service.

We store these in the push_subscriptions table, one row per device you enable push on. You can disable push from the profile panel; this removes the row from our database and tells the push service to stop accepting messages.

Communications

If you contact us by email, we store your email address and message content to respond and to send service-related communications such as updates about your suggestions or important changes to the service. You can opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data under the following legal bases (GDPR Article 6):

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — when you sign in with Google, you explicitly consent to us receiving and storing your profile information. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — we process usage data, venue suggestions, and edits to operate, improve, and secure the service. This includes analysing how the service is used, preventing abuse, and providing relevant content.
  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) — where we provide services you have requested, such as processing venue suggestions or delivering notifications you have opted into.

Promoted content and venue partnerships

Some venues may pay to be featured or promoted within the app (e.g. highlighted pins, featured listings). Promoted content is always clearly labelled as such.

We may share anonymised, aggregated statistics with venue partners — such as the number of times their listing was viewed or clicked. We never share your personal identity, account details, or individual browsing history with venue partners.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or serve behavioural ads.
  • We do not share your identity or browsing history with venue partners.
  • We do not store your precise GPS coordinates on our servers.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use the following types of cookies and local storage:

  • Strictly necessary — session cookies set by Supabase Auth for login. These are required for the service to function and are exempt from consent requirements under the ePrivacy Directive.
  • Preferences — local storage entries for your language and temperature unit settings.
  • Analytics — we may use first-party analytics cookies or privacy-focused analytics services to collect the usage data described above. Where required, we will ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

Third-party services

The app uses the following external services, each with their own privacy terms:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, realtime, and push fan-out (EU region). Privacy policy
  • Mapbox — map tiles and rendering. Your IP address is exposed to Mapbox when tiles are loaded. Privacy policy
  • Google Places — venue suggestions in the search box. Search queries are forwarded to Google’s Places API. Privacy policy
  • Yr / MET Norway — weather forecast data. No personal data is sent.
  • Google OAuth — sign-in only. We do not access your Google data beyond the profile fields listed above.
  • Apple Push, Google FCM, Mozilla autopush — your browser’s push service, used to deliver push notifications if you enable them.

We may integrate additional analytics or communication services in the future. Any new third-party service that processes personal data will be listed here and, where required, subject to your consent.

International data transfers

Your account data is stored in Supabase's EU region. However, some third-party services (Mapbox, Google) are based in the United States. These transfers are safeguarded by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as applicable.

Data retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all personal data is removed within 30 days.

Check-ins auto-expire and are deleted three hours after creation.

Push subscription records are deleted as soon as you disable push notifications or sign out from the device.

Analytics events that are tied to a user ID are deleted with your account. Analytics events that are not tied to a user (logged-out sessions) are retained indefinitely in pseudonymised form for service analysis.

Venue suggestions you withdraw are marked as withdrawn and excluded from the public dataset but may be kept for audit purposes.

How to delete your Shades account

You can permanently delete your Shades account and all associated data at any time, directly in the app:

  1. Open Shades and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile avatar (top-left) to open the profile panel.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap “Slett konto” (Delete account).
  4. Confirm in the dialog. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Prefer not to use the app? Email contact@findshades.app from the address tied to your account and we will delete it on your behalf.

What is deleted: your profile (name, email, avatar), favorites, sun alerts, friendships, plans you created, plan responses, check-ins, push subscriptions, venue suggestions, and all analytics events linked to your user ID. These are removed within 30 days of your request.

What is kept: analytics events that were never linked to your account (logged-out sessions) remain in pseudonymised form and cannot be traced back to you. Venue suggestions you submitted that were approved and added to the public map remain as venue data, with your personal identifier removed.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — delete your account and all associated data via your profile or by contacting us.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest, including analytics.
  • Withdraw consent — you can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Lodge a complaint — you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) at datatilsynet.no.
